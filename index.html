FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? We've got answers. Don't see yours? Give us a call.

A pigtail connector is a short wiring lead with a factory-matched connector on one end. When original wiring harness connectors get damaged — from heat, vibration, rodents, or corrosion — rather than replacing the entire harness, you splice in a pigtail to restore the original connection. It saves significant labor time and cost compared to full harness replacement.
Our pigtails are engineered to match OEM connector specifications for each manufacturer and engine series listed. If you're unsure about compatibility, contact us with your truck's year, make, model, and engine series and we'll confirm the right part for you before you order.
A bench harness allows you to run and test a diesel engine completely outside of the truck — on an engine stand or workbench. They're used by diesel rebuilders, engine shops, ECM programmers, and technicians who need to diagnose, test, or program an engine without it being installed in a vehicle.
